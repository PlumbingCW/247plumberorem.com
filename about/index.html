What Orem 24/7 Plumbing is

Orem 24/7 Plumbing is a dispatch service at 575 E University Pkwy in Orem, UT. You call (385) 488-1892, describe the problem and dispatch connects you with a licensed and insured plumber who performs the work.

We do not use online forms or chat bots. A plumbing emergency is a conversation, and the plumber needs to hear what is happening to bring the right parts. That is why every request starts with a phone call.

How the dispatch model works.

The plumbers who take your job are licensed and insured. Utah issues plumber licenses through the Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing in classes that run from apprentice to master, and you can verify any plumber's license on the state's online tool.

Dispatch matches the job with a plumber on duty near your address. The plumber explains the fix and gives you a clear price before work starts, and same-day appointments are offered when available. Because the work is done by the dispatched plumber, the invoice and the workmanship are between you and that plumber.

What you can expect from a call.

These are the commitments we make, and the ones we deliberately do not make.

  • 24/7 emergency dispatch, every day of the year.
  • Licensed and insured plumbers perform the work.
  • A clear price before work starts, not after.
  • Same-day appointments when available.
  • No promised arrival time, because traffic and active calls change it.
  • No online forms. You talk to a person.

Why we focus on Orem and Utah County.

Orem was incorporated in 1919. That makes it a young city, and most of its housing came later, which is why the age of your home matters more than the age of the city.

The city grew from 8,351 residents in 1950 to 52,399 in 1980, so most of its housing is now four to seven decades old. Knowing that age range tells a plumber which supply and drain materials to expect, which is why local knowledge shortens a diagnosis.

What happens after you call.

The process is short on purpose, because a plumbing problem usually has a clock on it.

  1. Dispatch asks for the basics

    Your address, what is happening and whether water is still running. Dispatch also gives safe first steps, such as where to find the main shutoff.

  2. A plumber is matched to the job

    The match depends on the type of problem and which licensed and insured plumbers are on duty near you.

  3. The plumber confirms the cause

    On site, the plumber checks the fixture or line, explains what failed and lays out repair options with a clear price before any work starts.

  4. The work is done and explained

    When the repair is finished, the plumber tells you what changed, what to watch and whether a permit or inspection applies.

What we do not claim.

We do not publish reviews, ratings or customer counts, because a number on a page is only worth something if it can be checked. We do not promise a fixed arrival time, because traffic and active calls change it hour by hour.

We also do not claim that every job is cheap. Some repairs, like a collapsed sewer line, are major work. What we promise is a clear price before work starts, so you decide with the number in front of you.

Where codes and permits fit in.

Plumbing work in Orem falls under the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as the city has adopted it. The Orem Building Safety Division handles permits, and permit rules vary by job, so ask the division when you are unsure.

The plumber you meet should be able to tell you whether your job needs a permit. If the answer sounds unsure, ask dispatch to confirm before work begins.

How to verify any plumber we send.

You should never have to take a plumber's word on credentials. Utah's Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing runs an online license verification tool, and it takes about a minute to search a name and see the license class and status.

Ask the plumber for the name on the license when they arrive, and check it if you like before work begins. A legitimate plumber expects the question.

Insurance is the other half. Licensed and insured plumbers carry coverage for the work they do in your home, and it is reasonable to ask about it. If anything about the answer feels vague, say so on the phone and dispatch will sort it out before the job starts.
