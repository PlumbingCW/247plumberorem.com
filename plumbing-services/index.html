What to do first before you call a plumber.

These steps protect people and property while dispatch finds a plumber.

  • Shut off the main water valve if water is escaping, and open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the lines.
  • Stop using any drain that is backing up, and keep children and pets out of the affected area.
  • Keep people away from wet electrical areas, and turn off power at the panel only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call anyone else.
  • Take photos of the damage before cleanup, for an insurance claim.
  • Have your address and the main valve location ready when dispatch answers.
